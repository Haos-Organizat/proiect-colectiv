05 / Motion

Moves once, then sits still.

Motion only confirms something happened. Nothing loops, pulses or breathes. Everything below is wrapped in prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference, and reduced motion gets the final state instantly.

  • Ticket reveal420msFade plus 16px rise, once, when the ticket enters view or right after purchase. Easing cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1).
  • Log in / register switch220msIncoming panel fades in with a 6px rise. Same ease-out. No exit animation.
  • Button and input states160msColor and border transitions only, no scale or bounce.
  • Theme switch0msInstant. Fading a whole page is slow and makes contrast flicker.

07 / Do and don't

Keep it cold and clean.

Do

  • Use cobalt for one thing per screen: the main action or the ticket.
  • Set headlines in Unbounded, big and tight, and give them space around.
  • Put every date, time, price and code in Geist Mono.
  • Use #2448E5 for links on light and #8FA5FF on dark.
  • Keep cards at 20px radius and buttons as full pills.
  • Show the QR on a white plate in both themes so scanners read it.

Don't

  • Don't set body text in cobalt #2F5BFF on the dark background, it fails at 3.78:1.
  • Don't add a second accent color or tint the neutrals navy.
  • Don't use gradients, glows or glass effects.
  • Don't put icons inside circles, boxes or colored tiles.
  • Don't animate anything in a loop, and never add live pulses.
  • Don't set Unbounded below 18px or for paragraphs, it gets too wide.
