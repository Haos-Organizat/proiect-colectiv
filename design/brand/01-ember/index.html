      07 Motion

      Four moments move. Everything else holds still.

      All motion lives inside @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference). With reduced motion, everything appears instantly. Every animation runs once; nothing loops.

      Where things animate

      1. Ticket reveal (My tickets, after purchase): fade plus 16px rise, 360ms, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1), once when it enters the viewport. Live on screen 04, use Replay to see it again.
      2. Log in / Register switch: panel fade plus 8px rise, 200ms, same easing. Live on screen 02.
      3. Form result (purchase or sign-in confirmation): opacity only, 200ms ease-out.
      4. Control feedback: background, border and color transitions on buttons, tags and inputs, 150ms ease. No scale, no bounce.

      Never

      • No infinite animations: no pulsing "live" dots, breathing badges, spinners on idle, marquees.
      • No parallax, scroll-jacking or reveal-on-scroll for regular content.
      • No animated theme switch; the toggle swaps tokens instantly.
      • Loading uses static skeleton blocks or a text label, not shimmer.

      08 Do / Don't

      Keep it a poster, not a casino.

      Do

      • Use ember for one thing per view: the main action or the key word in a headline.
      • Set headlines in Archivo 800 to 900, tight leading, and let them run big on mobile too.
      • Put dates, prices and ticket codes in IBM Plex Mono so numbers align and read as data.
      • Use primary-text (#A8330B) whenever ember is text on a light surface.
      • Keep dark ink labels on ember buttons in both themes.
      • Keep QR codes black on white in both themes so scanners at the door always read them.
      • Show errors inline, under the field, with text, not just a red border.

      Don't

      • Don't put white text on ember (3.0:1 fails contrast).
      • Don't add a second accent color or gradients; contrast comes from black, cream and size.
      • Don't box icons in circles, squares or tinted chips.
      • Don't use pure #000 or #FFF for surfaces; the blacks and creams are warm.
      • Don't animate lists, cards or headings on scroll.
      • Don't set inputs below 16px, iOS will zoom the page.
      • Don't use em dashes or bullet characters in product copy.
